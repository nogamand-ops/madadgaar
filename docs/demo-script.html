Help, when you need it. — Demo day script
6 min target (5–7 window) 2 presenters 3 apps, 1 backend
Before you walk in
Person A — Customer + opening/closing
Person B — Helper + Admin/business
The script
0:00
Person A

"Ali is driving home in Islamabad. He runs out of petrol — on the side of the road, at night, no idea who to call. That's the moment Madadgaar exists for. Not an Uber-for-petrol gimmick — Pakistan's on-demand roadside assistance platform. Fuel today, towing and mechanics too, and eventually anything you need help with, fast and verified."

0:35
Person A

"Here's Ali's phone."

Open customer_app → log in as Ali (+92 300 1234567, code is always 1234 in demo mode) → land on Home.
What judges see: real seeded data — "Trusted helpers near you" already lists Ahmed Hussain, 4.9★, verified, live from the backend, not hardcoded.
0:55
Person A

"One button. I need help."

Tap 🚨 I NEED HELP → "What's wrong?" → I'm out of fuel → Petrol, 5L → Continue.
1:15
Person A

"Real location — Madadgaar uses OpenStreetMap, no fake pin. And the price, before he commits to anything, broken all the way down. Nothing hidden."

Confirm the pre-filled location (F-7 Markaz, Islamabad) → Confirm → point at the breakdown: fuel cost, assistance fee, distance fee, night surcharge, total in PKR → Request Help.
1:45
Person A

"Finding a Madadgaar nearby… checking… and there he is."

Let the matching animation play (~2–4s) until the "Best match found" card appears.
What judges see: Ahmed Hussain, 4.9★, 412 jobs, Honda CD 70, verified — with his real distance and ETA computed live, not scripted.
2:15
Person B

"And that request just landed — live — on Ahmed's phone, in a completely separate app, over here."

Switch to helper_app (already logged in, online) → incoming request card is showing with a 15-second countdown and what Ahmed earns on this job → tap Accept.
This is the proof point: two independent apps, same backend, real-time WebSocket — not a mocked screen.
2:45
Person B

"Ahmed's heading over — live map, moving marker, same as the customer sees on their side."

On helper_app: tap through I've Arrived → Start Service → Complete Job (compress these, narrate while tapping).
3:15
Person A

"Back on Ali's side — job done, pay however's normal here: cash, Easypaisa, JazzCash. Then rate your Madadgaar."

Switch to customer_app → payment screen → pick JazzCash → Pay → 5 stars → Submit.
3:45
Person B

"Now the part that makes this a business, not just an app. Every request we just created shows up here, live, for Madadgaar."

Switch to admin_dashboard → point at Active Requests, Online Helpers, Today's Revenue, Commission.
4:15
Person B

"We take 10 to 15% commission plus a small service fee — never on the fuel cost itself, only on the assistance. Scroll down: GMV, helper payouts, take rate, all computed from real completed jobs, not made up."

Scroll to Business Metrics → GMV, Madadgaar Revenue, Helper Payouts, Take Rate.
5:00
Both

"We're launching in Islamabad and Rawalpindi — low-trust-barrier, high-density, easy to verify helpers fast. Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar next. Fuel delivery is the hook because everyone immediately gets it, but the platform is built for any roadside need — and eventually, any on-demand help. Madadgaar hai na."

5:40
Both

Stop talking. Let the last screen sit. Open the floor.
